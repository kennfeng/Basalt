How It Works

Basalt searches in two complementary ways, combines the results, and picks the best matches to build your answer.

How answers are built

Your question and the selected papers are combined into a focused prompt. The AI answers only from those papers and cites them. This keeps replies grounded and checkable. In tests, the final ranking step improved finding the right paper from 0.467 to 0.733, an increase of 57 percent, with typical speed of 1.85s median and 2.02s at 90th percentile on CPU.

Collection

250 research summaries from arXiv in AI, language, retrieval, vision, and machine learning. Stored locally on your device and searched together.

Area What it means

Key Terms Explained

Plain language definitions for the key components Basalt uses.

Term What it means
RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) A pattern where the AI first retrieves relevant documents and then generates an answer using only those documents. This reduces guessing and makes citations possible.
Vector Database A database that stores numerical representations of text so similar meanings can be found quickly, even if wording differs.
Embeddings Numerical vectors that capture the meaning of text. Similar ideas have similar vectors.
ChromaDB The local vector database Basalt uses to store and search embeddings on your device.
HNSW A fast graph-based index inside the vector database that makes nearest-neighbor search quick, even with many items.
BM25 A classic keyword search method that ranks documents by how often and how distinctively your words appear.
Hybrid Retrieval Using both keyword search (BM25) and meaning search (vectors) together to cover exact matches and semantic matches.
RRF (Reciprocal Rank Fusion) A simple way to blend two ranked lists by rewarding items that appear high in either list, then keeping the top results.
Cross-Encoder and Re-ranking A second model that reads your question and each candidate paper together to judge relevance more carefully, then re-orders the best matches.
Sentence-Transformers The family of models that turns sentences into embeddings, here all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (384 dimensions).
BAAI bge-reranker-base The specific cross-encoder model Basalt uses for the final ranking step.
LLM (Large Language Model) The AI that writes the final answer from the selected papers, here Ollama llama3.2:1b by default, or an OpenAI-compatible model.
FastAPI The web framework that serves the API your browser and apps talk to.
Docker A way to package and run the whole app the same way on any machine.
MRR and Hit Rate Measures of search quality. Hit Rate checks if the right answer is in the top 3. MRR rewards putting the right answer higher.
Latency (p50, p90, p95) Speed measures. p50 is the median, p90 and p95 show slower cases. Lower is faster.
